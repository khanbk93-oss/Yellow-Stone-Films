1. Definitions

  • Services: the production work described in the quotation, including pre-production, shooting, post-production and delivery.
  • Deliverables: the final edited films, photographs or other outputs listed in the quotation.
  • Raw Material: all unedited footage, photographs, audio, project files and working files.
  • Shoot Day: a continuous working period of up to 10 hours, including set-up and wrap, unless stated otherwise in the quotation.

2. Quotation and Scope of Work

  1. The quotation lists the scope, number of Shoot Days, crew, equipment, locations, Deliverables and timelines. Anything not listed is outside the scope.

  3. Any change to the scope (extra days, locations, crew, equipment, Deliverables or versions) must be agreed in writing and may change the price and schedule.

3. Booking and Advance Payment

  1. A booking is confirmed only after the Company receives the signed quotation or written confirmation and the advance payment.
  2. Unless otherwise stated in the quotation, the advance is 50% of the total fee, payable before the first Shoot Day or pre-production start.
  3. Dates are not held for the Client until the advance is received.

4. Payment Terms

  1. Unless otherwise stated in the quotation: 50% advance on booking, 30% before the final Shoot Day or on delivery of the first cut, and 20% balance before release of final files.
  2. Invoices are payable within 7 days of the invoice date unless stated otherwise.
  3. Late payments may attract interest at 2% per month on the outstanding amount.
  4. The Company may pause work or withhold Deliverables, in whole or in part, until overdue amounts are paid.
  5. Payments are made by bank transfer, UPI or cheque in favour of the account named on the invoice.

5. Cancellation and Rescheduling

  1. By the Client: If the Client cancels or postpones, the advance is non-refundable. In addition:

    • 7 to 15 days before: 50% of the total fee is payable.
    • Less than 7 days before: 100% of the total fee is payable, as crew and equipment will have been committed.
  2. Rescheduling: One reschedule with at least 7 days' notice is allowed without penalty, subject to crew and equipment availability. Further reschedules may incur a fee.
  3. By the Company: If the Company must cancel for reasons within its control, it will offer an alternate date or refund amounts paid for work not yet performed.

  1. Provide timely access to locations, people, schedules and information needed for the shoot.
  2. Obtain all permissions, permits, location releases and consents required for filming at the locations and of the people involved, unless the quotation states the Company will do so.
  3. Provide a single point of contact authorised to make decisions and give approvals.
  4. Provide safe working conditions, power access, parking and, where agreed, meals and drinking water for the crew.
  5. Ensure that any materials supplied to the Company (logos, music, footage, scripts, images) are owned by the Client or properly licensed.

8. Overtime and Extra Days

  1. Work beyond the agreed Shoot Day will be charged as overtime at the rate stated in the quotation. If no rate is stated, overtime is charged at 10% of the day rate per additional hour.
  2. Waiting time caused by the Client or the venue counts as working time.
  3. Early call times, night shoots and work on public holidays may carry additional charges.

9. Travel, Accommodation and Expenses

  1. Unless included in the quotation, the Client pays for travel, local transport, accommodation, meals, permits, location fees and similar costs for the crew.

  1. The quotation states the number of edited versions and rounds of revisions included. Typically this is two rounds of consolidated feedback on each cut.
  2. Feedback must be sent in writing, consolidated from all stakeholders, within 5 days of receiving each cut. Delays in feedback may move the delivery timeline.
  3. Additional revisions, re-edits, new versions, formats, aspect ratios or changes to the approved cut after sign-off will be charged separately.
  4. Photographs are delivered colour-corrected and edited as agreed. Selection of images for editing is at the Company's creative discretion unless agreed otherwise.
  5. Approval by email or message from the Client's authorised contact is final approval of the cut or selection.

11. Delivery Timelines

  1. Timelines in the quotation begin from the later of the last Shoot Day and receipt of all required inputs and payments from the Client.
  2. Delivery is by digital transfer (download link or cloud storage). Physical media may be charged extra.
  3. Delays caused by late feedback, late payments, missing materials or changes in scope extend the timeline accordingly.
  4. Digital files will be available for download for 30 days after delivery. The Client is responsible for making its own backups.
